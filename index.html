1. Summary

  • The App has no accounts or sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers such as the Advertising ID.
  • Tips and scores are loaded from our servers (Google Firebase). A small setting is stored on your device.
  • The App is for adults aged 18 and over, and is for information and entertainment only.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

We do not collect personal information that you type in, because the App has no registration, profile or comment features. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and we use them only to reply.

2.2 Information collected automatically by third-party services

When you use the App, the services described in section 4 may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your device settings)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and screen size
  • Network information, such as IP address (which gives an approximate location, for example country or city) and connection type
  • Usage and diagnostic data, such as ad impressions, ad interactions and technical errors

We do not use this information to identify you personally.

2.3 Information stored on your device

The App stores a small preference on your device, for example whether you have already seen the welcome screens. This stays on your device and is removed when you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

2.4 Permissions

The App uses internet and network-state access to load tips and scores and to show ads. It also declares the Advertising ID permission used by Google’s advertising services. The App does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, files or precise location.

3. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the App, including loading predictions, tips and scores
  • To show ads, which keep the App free, and to measure and limit how often ads are shown
  • To keep the App secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix technical problems
  • To understand how the App is used so we can improve it
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising and your choices

Ads in the App are provided by Google AdMob and may be personalized or non-personalized. You can control advertising in these ways:

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Opt out of ad personalization: the same Ads settings screen, or Google Ad Settings.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: where required by law, the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later where the App offers a privacy settings option.

Some features, such as unlocking VIP tips, are available by watching a rewarded ad. Watching is always optional.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

7. Retention and deletion

We do not hold personal accounts or profiles, so there is no account to delete. Data collected by Google services is kept according to Google’s own policies. Data stored on your device can be removed at any time by clearing the App’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the App. To ask us about any personal data we may hold (for example, an email you sent us), see section 12.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) when the App communicates with our servers and Google services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not collect data that identifies you directly, most requests can be handled by the controls in section 5. To make a request, contact us using the details in section 12.

10. Predictions and gambling disclaimer

Predictions, tips, statistics and analysis in the App are for information and entertainment only. They are estimates, not guarantees, and past results do not predict future ones. The App does not accept bets, handle money or process payments, and it is not a gambling service.

If you choose to gamble elsewhere, you must be of legal age in your country (18+ in most places) and should only stake what you can afford to lose. If gambling is a problem for you or someone you know, please seek help from a local support organization.

Match data, team names and logos belong to their respective owners and are used for identification and information only. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, club or sports organization.
